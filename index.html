Lockstep

Grab the chips. Outrun the spiders. Reach the flag.

Every key opens one door of its color (the green key opens them all). Spiders sit boxed in behind chips and keys. Pick one up and they spot you, react half a second later, and chase at nearly your speed. No spider can cross a gate. The levels grow bigger than your screen, so remember what you've seen.

Field guide

    Play in landscape. Press a direction on the circle to step; hold to run; slide your thumb to turn. On a keyboard: ← ↑ → ↓ or WASD move (hold to run), 1–4 ready a power-up then pick a direction, Z undo, R restart, P pause.
