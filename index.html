Lockstep

Grab the chips. Outrun the spiders. Reach the flag.

Every key opens one door of its color. Spiders never stop coming for you, so anything in their way keeps them boxed in, until you pick it up. No spider can cross a gate. The levels grow bigger than your screen, so remember what you've seen. Nothing moves unless you do.

Field guide

    Play in landscape. Slide your thumb around the circle to move; tap its center to wait a turn. On a keyboard: ← ↑ → ↓ or WASD move, Space wait, 1–4 ready a power-up then pick a direction, Z undo, R restart, P pause.
