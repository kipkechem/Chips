Lockstep

Grab the chips. Wake the spiders. Reach the gate.

Every key opens one door of its color. Spiders sleep until they see you in a straight line, then chase you until something blocks them. They can never cross a gate, so plan your escape before you pick anything up. Nothing moves unless you do.
